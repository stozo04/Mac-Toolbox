# Audio

Record, clean up and convert audio

## Tools
- [Clean Up Audio](./Clean Up Audio/README.md) - Reduce background noise and hum in voice recordings and videos.
- [Record Audio](./Record Audio/README.md) - Record your voice, then transcribe it on device.
- [Trim Audio](./Trim Audio/README.md) - Cut a song or recording down to the part you want.
- [Extract Audio](./Extract Audio/README.md) - Save the soundtrack of a video as an audio file.
- [Convert Audio](./Convert Audio/README.md) - Change audio files to MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC or AIFF.
