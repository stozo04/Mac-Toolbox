# Images

Edit, convert and protect photos

## Tools
- [Blur Faces](./Blur Faces/README.md) - Find faces in photos and blur, pixelate or black them out.
- [Beautify Screenshot](./Beautify Screenshot/README.md) - Put screenshots on a soft background with padding, rounded corners and a shadow.
- [Remove Background](./Remove Background/README.md) - Lift the subject out of photos and save it with a transparent or solid background.
- [Convert Images](./Convert Images/README.md) - Save photos as HEIC, WebP, JPG, JPEG or PNG.
- [Compress Images](./Compress Images/README.md) - Make photos smaller without visible loss in quality.
- [Resize Images](./Resize Images/README.md) - Scale photos by percentage, longest side or exact size.
- [Rotate Images](./Rotate Images/README.md) - Rotate or flip many photos at once.
- [Remove Metadata](./Remove Metadata/README.md) - Strip location, camera and date info from photos.
- [Color Picker](./Color Picker/README.md) - Pick any color on screen and copy it as HEX, RGB, HSL or code.
- [Redact Image](./Redact Image/README.md) - Hide names, faces and private details in an image.
- [Annotate Screenshot](./Annotate Screenshot/README.md) - Add arrows, boxes, numbers and text to a screenshot.
