# Video

Record, trim, convert and shrink video

## Tools
- [Download Video](./Download Video/README.md) - Save videos from YouTube, X, TikTok and more.
- [Burn In Subtitles](./Burn In Subtitles/README.md) - Add captions to a video from an SRT file or automatic transcription.
- [Change Speed](./Change Speed/README.md) - Speed up or slow down videos without chipmunk voices.
- [Extract Frames](./Extract Frames/README.md) - Save stills from videos as PNG, JPEG or HEIC images.
- [Convert Video](./Convert Video/README.md) - Change videos to MP4, MOV, WebM or M4V.
- [Record Screen](./Record Screen/README.md) - Record a display, a window or an area, with sound.
- [Compress Video](./Compress Video/README.md) - Shrink video files while keeping them looking great.
- [Resize Video](./Resize Video/README.md) - Change the dimensions of videos to an exact size.
- [Crop & Rotate Video](./Crop & Rotate Video/README.md) - Crop, rotate and flip a video.
- [Media Info](./Media Info/README.md) - See the codec, resolution, frame rate, bitrate and metadata of videos and audio.
- [Trim Video](./Trim Video/README.md) - Cut the start and end of a clip without re-encoding.
- [Merge Videos](./Merge Videos/README.md) - Join several clips into one video, in the order you choose.
- [Mute Video](./Mute Video/README.md) - Remove the soundtrack from videos without re-encoding.
- [Replace Audio](./Replace Audio/README.md) - Swap or add the soundtrack of a video with another audio file.
- [Video to GIF](./Video to GIF/README.md) - Turn a clip into an animated GIF.
