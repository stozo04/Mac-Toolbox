# Docs

Scan, sign, convert and protect docs

## Tools
- [Scan Document](./Scan Document/README.md) - Scan paper with your iPhone or from photos. Pages are found, straightened and cleaned up.
- [Redact PDF](./Redact PDF/README.md) - Black out names, numbers or whole areas so they're removed for good, not just covered up.
- [Fill & Flatten Forms](./Fill & Flatten Forms/README.md) - Fill in PDF forms, add text where there are no fields, and flatten the answers so they print as-is.
- [Make PDF Searchable](./Make PDF Searchable/README.md) - Add an invisible text layer to scanned PDFs so you can search, select and copy their text.
- [PDF to Word](./PDF to Word/README.md) - Turn PDFs into Word documents you can edit, with headings, lists and paragraphs.
- [Sign PDF](./Sign PDF/README.md) - Draw or type your signature and place it on a document.
- [Merge PDFs](./Merge PDFs/README.md) - Combine several PDFs and images into one document.
- [Split PDF](./Split PDF/README.md) - Break a PDF into single pages or page ranges.
- [Organize PDF](./Organize PDF/README.md) - Reorder, rotate and remove pages in a PDF.
- [Delete Pages](./Delete Pages/README.md) - Remove the pages you don't need from a PDF.
- [Extract Pages](./Extract Pages/README.md) - Save selected pages as a new PDF.
- [Images to PDF](./Images to PDF/README.md) - Turn photos and scans into a single PDF document.
- [Compress PDF](./Compress PDF/README.md) - Make PDFs smaller by recompressing their images.
