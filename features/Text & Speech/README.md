# Text & Speech

Transcribe, translate and summarize, privately

## Tools
- [Transcribe](./Transcribe/README.md) - Turn speech in audio and video into a timestamped transcript.
- [Summarize](./Summarize/README.md) - Summarize text and documents on device with Apple Intelligence.
- [Extract Text](./Extract Text/README.md) - Recognize text in images, screenshots and scanned PDFs.
- [Capture Text](./Capture Text/README.md) - Select any area of the screen and copy the text in it.
- [Translate](./Translate/README.md) - Translate text, documents and subtitles on device, keeping their formatting and timing.
- [Text to Speech](./Text to Speech/README.md) - Turn text, documents and PDFs into natural speech, and save it as audio.
- [Ask a Document](./Ask a Document/README.md) - Ask questions about a PDF or document and get answers with page references.
