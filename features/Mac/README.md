# Mac

Free up space and fix what is stuck

## Tools
- [Network Speed Test](./Network Speed Test/README.md) - Measure download, upload and responsiveness with Apple's own network test.
- [Storage Explorer](./Storage Explorer/README.md) - See what's filling your disk, find the usual space hogs and clear them out safely.
- [Wi-Fi Password & QR](./Wi-Fi Password & QR/README.md) - Show the password of a saved Wi-Fi network and make a QR code guests can scan to join.
- [Battery Health](./Battery Health/README.md) - See your Mac's battery capacity, cycle count and charger, plus AirPods and accessory levels.
- [Keep Awake](./Keep Awake/README.md) - Stop your Mac from sleeping during downloads, renders or talks, for as long as you choose.
- [Uninstall App](./Uninstall App/README.md) - Remove an app together with the caches, preferences and data it leaves behind.
- [Presentation Mode](./Presentation Mode/README.md) - One switch before you share your screen: hide desktop icons, hide the Dock and stay awake.
- [Clean Keyboard](./Clean Keyboard/README.md) - Pause the keyboard for a minute so you can wipe the keys without typing gibberish.
- [Eject All Disks](./Eject All Disks/README.md) - Safely eject every external drive, card and disk image at once before you unplug.
- [Login & Background Items](./Login & Background Items/README.md) - See what starts with your Mac and runs in the background, who made it, and turn off what you don't need.
- [Fix "App Is Damaged"](./Fix App Is Damaged/README.md) - Check who made an app that won't open, then clear the download quarantine so macOS lets it run.
- [Reset Permissions](./Reset Permissions/README.md) - Unstick an app's Screen Recording, Microphone, Camera or other privacy access.
- [Hidden Settings](./Hidden Settings/README.md) - Switch on useful macOS settings that aren't in System Settings, like a faster Dock or hidden files.
- [Display Control](./Display Control/README.md) - Change the brightness, contrast and volume of external monitors, right from your Mac.
- [Mouse Fixes](./Mouse Fixes/README.md) - Scroll your mouse and trackpad in different directions, and make the mouse wheel faster.
